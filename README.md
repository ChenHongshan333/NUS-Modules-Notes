# Notes for NUS modules I have taken before

I am a CS student enrolled in 2024 in NUS. I am the kind of person who gets used to take notes by pen-and-paper, but it's quite frustrating to organize all the written materials at every end of one semester.

That is the reason why I come up with this repo: a digital copy of what I have written down, including lecture notes, some past year papers, and cheatsheets.
